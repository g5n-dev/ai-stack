> As companies of various sizes adopt graphic processing units \(GPU\)-based machine learning \(ML\) training, fine-tuning and inference workloads, the demand for GPU capacity has outpaced industry-wide supply . This imbalance has made GPUs a scarce resource , creating a challenge for customers who need reliable access to GPU compute resources for their ML workloads.
>
> When you encounter GPU capacity limitations, you might consider creating on-demand capacity reservations \(ODCRs\) . ODCRs apply to planned, steady-state workloads with well-understood usage patterns. Short-term ODCR availability for GPU instances, particularly P-type instances, is often limited. Additionally, without a long-term contract, ODCRs are billed at on-demand rates, offering no cost advantage.…
