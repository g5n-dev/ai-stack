So if it finishes compiling with no extra axioms, it is probably correct, and no human will ever have read all of it…

Both AI-claimed Millennium Prize results will take years to verify.

> Claimed Proof of the Riemann Hypothesis with Claude
>
> The claim: Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge says that, "working with Claude," he has found a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis. He says the method also covers the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis (GRH) (Scientific American, statement summary, remark.pdf link).
>
> Artifact: Only Lean code was released. A written-out prose version reportedly ran past 250,000 pages (timeline).
>
> Timeline: The work began on Oct 6. Eight of the 20 steps landed on Nov 15, and the final entry on Nov 16 reads "GRH." Alpöge credits Claude for the parts involving automorphic forms on higher-rank groups, an area where he says he has little mastery (breakdown).
>
> Unknowns: The Claude model used and the degree of human steering have not been disclosed (open questions).
>
> Verification status: Elliot Glazer confirms that the formalized statement is GRH. The Lean code is "still compiling, will take a few hours." He puts the chance of a Lean bug at &lt;&lt;1% (analysis).
>
> Provisional status: Artificial Analysis notes that both AI-claimed Millennium results are unverified: this one and OpenAI's September Navier-Stokes claim, which used an unreleased model (tally).
>
> Competing human proof: Alessandro Masullo has announced a separate, purely human proof of RH. It is reported as a roughly 940-page GRH draft (announcement, page count).
>
> Interpretation debate: Some argue the result shows we have little idea how hard math problems objectively are, rather than showing broad superhuman ability (Theo Jaffee, Ege Erdil).
>
> Brute-force framing: Others compare the approach to olympiad-style "bashing" at a scale humans lack the willpower for (cloneofsimo).
>
> Jaggedness: Omar Khattab notes the contrast between solving Millennium problems and being "this dumb on 1000 other things" (post).
>
> Market reaction: ANT stock fell about 10% on the day (scaling01, Nabeel Qureshi).
