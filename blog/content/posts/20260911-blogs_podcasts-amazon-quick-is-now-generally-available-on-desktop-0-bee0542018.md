> Your teams get an AI assistant that handles real work while your data stays in your environment and your conversations stay private
>
> Today, the Amazon Quick desktop application is generally available on macOS and Windows. We’re also adding a new activity feed to the mobile experience on iOS and Android that consolidates email, calendar, CRM, and messaging into one prioritized view, showing your teams the decisions that need them while agents resolve the routine items in the background, so they spend less time sorting through updates and more time on high-value work.
>
> Enterprise IT organizations face a compounding problem: the tools your teams need to stay productive are multiplying, and so is the shadow AI risk that comes with them. When people work around approved systems to get things done faster, governance breaks down, data leaves controlled environments, and IT loses visibility. The answer is not to restrict AI access but to give teams an enterprise-grade AI assistant built for work, on infrastructure IT already controls. Quick runs on AWS, the same infrastructure behind the most security-sensitive workloads on the planet. Your data stays in your environment, your conversations remain private, and everything is auditable. Full audit trails are available through Amazon CloudWatch and AWS CloudTrail. The compliance certifications your security team requires (HIPAA, FedRAMP, SOC 2, and ISO 27001) are built in from day one.
>
> Customers across manufacturing, healthcare, and sports have been using Quick on desktop during preview—asking complex questions in natural language and getting grounded, trustworthy answers in seconds, cutting time-to-insight significantly while keeping governance and accuracy built in from the start.
>
> For organizations managing knowledge workers across hundreds of roles and workflows, the capacity equation matters too. Business goals keep growing, but the hours in a day do not. Most of the time that should go to high-judgment work gets consumed by pulling information together, chasing follow-ups, and assembling deliverables. Quick changes that equation by providing a thought partner that knows your context, builds what you need, and takes action on your behalf. Quick also gives your team a shared workspace where the dashboards, agents, and automations one person builds are available to the whole team.

> Focus on what matters, not what’s loudest
>
> Most work tools generate a firehose of notifications. Quick’s activity feed does the opposite: it consolidates signals from email, messaging, CRM, and calendar into one prioritized view. Items agents resolve on their own disappear. What remains is a short queue of things only you can act on, and the feed gets smarter over time. Quick learns your relationships, priorities, and patterns, so it surfaces what matters most, not just what arrived most recently.
