## 来源摘要/节选

> We’re excited to share that AWS has been recognized as a Leader in The Forrester Wave: AI Infrastructure Solutions, Q4 2025. In this evaluation of 13 providers, AWS received the highest score in the Strategy category.
>
> We believe this recognition reflects our commitment to delivering flexible, cost-efficient AI infrastructure that helps you move from experimentation to production-ready AI at scale.
>
> The growing importance of AI infrastructure
>
> As AI adoption accelerates, you need infrastructure that supports the full lifecycle of AI workloads—from training and fine-tuning to inference at scale.
>
> According to Forrester’s evaluation, successful AI infrastructure requires three elements:
>
> Workload-specific architecture tailored for training, fine-tuning, and inference rather than generic computational needs.
>
> Engineering efficiency with coordinated design across compute, network, storage, and orchestration to maximize utilization.
>
> Sustainability and flexibility addressing power, cooling, and avoiding vendor lock-in.
>

> How AWS approaches AI infrastructure
>
> Our approach to AI infrastructure centers on customer choice and cost efficiency. We offer both proprietary AI chips, AWS Inferentia and AWS Trainium, alongside NVIDIA GPU instances. With both options, you can match the right compute to each workload without lock-in.
>
> As Forrester noted in the report: “AWS’ vision is to offer both proprietary accelerators and NVIDIA GPUs, giving customers cost-efficient options for inference workloads without hardware lock-in. Its custom silicon delivers strong performance for mainstream tasks, while NVIDIA supports high-end needs. The compelling roadmap signals continued investment in silicon development and closer integration with AWS services such as Amazon SageMaker. AWS partners closely with leading model developers, giving customers direct access to a diverse set of foundation models.”
>
> Forrester also observed: “AWS is best for companies with high-volume or elastic inference workloads that prioritize cost efficiency but still need access to GPUs for peak performance scenarios.”
>
