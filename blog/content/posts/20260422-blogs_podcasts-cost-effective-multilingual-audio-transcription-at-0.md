> Many organizations are archiving large media libraries, analyzing contact center recordings, preparing training data for AI, or processing on-demand video for subtitles. When data volumes grow significantly, managed automatic speech recognition \(ASR\) service costs can quickly become the primary constraint on scalability.
>
> To address this cost-scalability challenge, we use the NVIDIA Parakeet-TDT-0.6B-v3 model, deployed through AWS Batch on GPU-accelerated instances. Parakeet-TDT’s Token-and-Duration Transducer architecture simultaneously predicts text tokens and their duration to intelligently skip silence and redundant processing. This helps achieve inference speeds orders of magnitude faster than real-time.…
