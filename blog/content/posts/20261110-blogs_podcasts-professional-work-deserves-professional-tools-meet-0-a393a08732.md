## 要点解读

### 这是什么
AWS Quick 是面向企业知识工作者的 AI 工作空间，可让 AI 智能体跨企业现有数据与应用进行研究、分析和执行操作。此次内容还介绍了其品牌更名、企业级安全治理能力及面向美国和英国知识工作者的推广活动。

### 用在哪里
它适用于需要把 CRM、仪表板、通话记录、邮件及企业应用中的信息用于团队协作的场景，尤其面向 IT 负责人、销售代表、项目经理和财务负责人。支持通过自然语言创建智能体、应用与自动化，并可接入 Microsoft Purview、AWS IAM、Microsoft Active Directory 等权限与合规体系。

### 可以推断的
- 推测：Quick 的主要差异化方向是让个人效率转化为团队和企业层面的可复用生产力。
- 推测：企业采用这类工具时，权限继承、数据隔离、审计追踪和审批机制可能比生成内容本身更关键。

The campaign celebrates the teams who know that the work they do every day deserves tools built for it.

> The work you know
>
> Customers tell us their AI assistants make individuals faster, but the gains stay personal. A sales rep still builds territory plans by pulling from the CRM, dashboards, call notes, and email. Because context stays locked inside a single project or one vendor’s suite, productivity never compounds across the team or the company. Most of those tools started as consumer apps and still serve personal and professional use with the same product. AWS Quick took the opposite path: it was built for work from day one, on the same AWS infrastructure that millions of customers use to run their mission-critical applications. These include many of the world’s most complex and security-sensitive workloads.
>
> Built for your business from day one
>
> AWS Quick has no consumer version. Every roadmap decision and every feature we ship starts with one question: does it help individuals, teams, and entire enterprises do their most important work? Quick knows your role, your team, and how your projects, people, and data connect, because it works across the applications and workflows your teams already use. Quick watches for changes, surfaces what matters, and acts on your behalf, which leaves your team free to focus on what moves the business forward. Anyone can build agents, apps, and automations by describing what they need in plain language, without filing an IT ticket. Everything built in a shared space becomes an asset the whole team can use.
>
> Trust built in
>
> Quick is integrated with the security and compliance tools you already use, including Microsoft Purview for data protection, AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM), and Microsoft Active Directory for permission-based controls. Quick reads the sensitivity labels already on your files, enforces existing permissions, and flags potentially sensitive data to administrators. Assets including agents, knowledge bases, and skills reach new teams only through approved sharing, and every approval and denial is recorded with who decided, what they shared, and why. Quick also carries the compliance certifications most organizations require, including HIPAA, FedRAMP, SOC 2, and ISO 27001. Your data is yours, and your conversations are private. No one else can browse your conversations, and IT admins cannot access your chat transcripts. Amazon CloudWatch and AWS CloudTrail are built in, giving your organization a full audit trail of what happened, who did it, and when. Your data never leaves your environment and never trains someone else’s models.
